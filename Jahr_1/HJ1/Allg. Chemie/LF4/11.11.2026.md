## pH-wert Bestimmung

- H2O + H2O -> H3O⁺ + OH-

**Formel:**
Kw = (cH3O) * c(OH-) / c²(H2O+)
pH = -lg10 c(H3O+)
c(H3O+) = 10^-ph
pOH = -lg c(OH-)
c(OH-) = 10^-pOH
