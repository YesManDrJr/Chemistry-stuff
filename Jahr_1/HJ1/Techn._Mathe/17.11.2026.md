## Massenkonzentration

B(x) = m(X) / V
m(x) = B(x) * V

**1g/cm^3 = 1Kg/L**

A2.
geg B1(NaCl) = 283 g/L, B2(NaCl) = 311 g/l, V = 1.50m^3 = 1500 L

ges: m(NaCl)

DELTA_B(NaCl) = 311 g/L - 283 g/L = 28 g/L

m(NaCl) = DELTA_B * V
m(NaCl) = 28 g/L * 1500L = 42.000 g = 42,0 Kg (Signifikanz)
