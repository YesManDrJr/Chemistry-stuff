    ~/operation-privacy

    Operation: Privacy

    A personal initiative focused on digital privacy, information safety, and helping people think more critically about how their data is collected, used, stored, and shared.

    Our plan

    • Raise awareness about the lack of digital privacy and general information safety.
    • Encourage people to question whether mass surveillance actually makes them safer.
    • Promote the idea that personal data should remain under the control of the people it belongs to, rather than being treated as a commodity.
    • Help people become more aware of their digital footprint and the services they rely on.
    • Your history is not the profit of another company, Your transactions are not entertainment for surveillance.

    Why it matters

    Everyday digital activities create information about our habits, interests, relationships, and behaviour. Understanding what happens to that information is an important part of using technology responsibly.

  • They say mass surveillance is safety, but in reality its just another nice word sold as a indirect lie.
  • They think it makes the internet safer, while they spy on even the innocent ones.

    • Core principles

    • Privacy is a right, not a privilege. But they sell privacy as danger and insecurity.
    • People should be able to make informed choices about their data.
    • Transparency and accountability matter.
    • Security should protect people, not become an excuse for unnecessary data collection.
    • They may play us by collecting massive unecessary data for their own profit, but the more people are aware, the harder it will get for them.

    What people can do

    Learn about privacy settings, review app permissions, use strong and unique passwords, keep software updated, and think carefully before sharing personal information online.

  • We tend to call out people to join the monero or tari network for more safety and privacy in each transaction
  • NOTE: Each of these 2 crypto currencies has their own privacy mechanics and features. Please inform yourself before participating if you want to know how these projects contribute into privacy.

    • PRIVACY IS A RIGHT. NOT A PRIVILEGE.
